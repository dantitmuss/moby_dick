Privacy policy

Last updated · April 2025

The Moby-Dick Bible respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, and what we don’t.

1. Cookies & tracking

We use cookies to understand how visitors use the site. These are only activated when you give consent.

2. Google Tag Manager

We use GTM to manage site scripts. It doesn’t collect personal information on its own.

3. Google Analytics (if enabled)

When active, Google Analytics collects anonymized data like which pages you visit, how long you stay, and your browser type. Your IP address is anonymized. We do not collect any personally identifiable information.

4. Your questions

What you type is sent to OpenAI to write your verse and find the matching passages. If you accept analytics cookies, your questions may also be recorded in our analytics.

5. The ship’s log

Verses you choose to keep are stored in your own browser, on this device only. They never reach us. Clearing your browser’s site data removes them.

6. Your control

You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing cookies in your browser settings.

7. Questions?

Contact us at dantitmusscomedy@gmail.com.
